# How to Book the Hall

This guide walks you through making a room booking on the hall website. No technical knowledge is needed.

---

## Before you start

You will need:

- The **address of the hall website** (your hall administrator can give you this).
- Your **name and email address**.
- The **booking password**, if the hall uses one (your administrator will give it to you).

---

## Step 1 — Go to the calendar

Open the hall website in your browser and go to the calendar page.

You will see a monthly calendar showing existing bookings. Dates that are partially or fully booked show coloured bars or dots. Empty dates are available.

---

## Step 2 — Start a new booking

**Option A — from the calendar:**
Find the date you want. If it is in the future and not fully booked, you will see a small **Book** button on that day. Click it.

**Option B — from the booking form page:**
If the hall has a separate booking page, go straight to that page instead.

The booking form will open with your chosen date already filled in (if you used Option A).

---

## Step 3 — Fill in the form

Work through each section in order.

---

### Password *(only shown if the hall requires one)*

Enter the password your hall administrator gave you. If you do not know it, contact your administrator before continuing.

---

### Room & Time

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Select Room(s)** | Tick the room or rooms you need. Each room shows its capacity in brackets. You can tick more than one. |
| **Booking Date** | Type the date or click the calendar icon to pick it. You cannot book a date in the past. |
| **Start Time** | Enter the time your booking begins, e.g. `19:00` for 7 pm. |
| **End Time** | Enter the time your booking ends. This must be after the start time. |

> **Conflict warning:** If the room is already booked for any part of your chosen time, a yellow warning message will appear. Choose a different time or room.

---

### Your Information

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Your Name** | Enter your full name. |
| **Your Email** | Enter the email address where you want your confirmation sent. |
| **Select Group** | If your lodge or organisation is listed, select it. This is optional but helps the hall keep records. If you are not sure, leave it on *— Select a Group (Optional) —*. |
| **Event Category** | Choose the type of event from the list, e.g. *Lodge Meeting*, *Dinner*, *Committee Meeting*. This is required. |

---

### Additional Details

| Field | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Purpose of Booking** | A short title for the event, e.g. *Regular Lodge Meeting* or *Annual Dinner*. |
| **Description** | Any extra information for the hall administrator — e.g. special room layout, expected number of guests. This is optional. |
| **Attach File** | If you need to share a document (PDF only, maximum 5 MB), click *Choose File* and select it. This is optional. |

---

### Repeating bookings *(skip this section for a one-off booking)*

If the same event happens regularly (e.g. every month), you can set it up as one repeating booking instead of entering each date separately.

**To set up a repeating booking:**

1. Tick **Repeat this booking**.
2. Under **Repeat**, choose how often it repeats:
   - *Daily* — every day
   - *Weekly* — same day every week
   - *Every 2 Weeks* — fortnightly
   - *Monthly (Same Date)* — e.g. the 15th of every month
   - *Monthly (Same Weekday)* — e.g. the third Wednesday of every month
3. Under **Repeat Until**, enter the last date you want the booking to run to.

**To book a list of specific dates that do not follow a regular pattern:**

1. Tick **Book multiple specific dates**.
2. Click **+ Add Another Date** for each extra date you need.
3. All dates will use the same start and end time you entered above.

---

### Terms and Conditions *(only shown if the hall uses these)*

Read the terms, then tick the box to confirm you agree. You cannot submit the form without ticking this.

---

## Step 4 — Submit your booking

Click the **Submit Booking** button at the bottom of the form.

If anything is missing or incorrect, a message will appear at the top of the form explaining what to fix. Correct the field and try again.

If the submission is successful, you will see:

> *"Your booking has been submitted successfully! You will receive a confirmation email once it is approved."*

The page will return to the calendar after a few seconds.

---

## What happens next

1. Your booking is saved as **Pending** — it is not yet confirmed.
2. The hall administrator receives an email and will review it.
3. Once approved, you will receive a **second email** telling you the booking is confirmed.

If you do not hear back within a reasonable time, contact the hall administrator directly.

---

## Your confirmation email

After submitting, you will receive an email with your booking details. **Keep this email** — it contains two important personal links:

| Link | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Update your booking** | Opens a form where you can change the event name, date, time, or your contact details — no login needed. |
| **Cancel this booking** | Opens a page where you can cancel the booking — no login needed. |

These links are unique to your booking. Anyone with the link can use it, so do not share the email publicly.

---

## Changing your booking

1. Open the confirmation email.
2. Click **Update your booking** (or the link labelled "Need to make changes? Update your booking here").
3. A form opens showing your current booking details.
4. Change whatever you need — event name, description, date, time, or contact details.
5. Click **Save Changes**.

The hall administrator will automatically receive an email showing what you changed.

**Important notes:**
- If you change the date or time, the system will check for conflicts and warn you if the room is already taken at the new time.
- The room cannot be changed through this form. Contact the hall administrator if you need a different room.

---

## Cancelling your booking

1. Open the confirmation email.
2. Click **Cancel this booking** (or the link labelled "Need to cancel? Use this link").
3. The page shows your booking details so you can confirm it is the right one.
4. Click **Yes, Cancel My Booking**.

The hall administrator is notified automatically. If you change your mind, click **No, Keep My Booking** instead.

---

## Setting up a signing-in sheet for your event *(for dinners and formal meetings)*

For events such as lodge dinners, you can set up a **booking-in form** — a simple online form that your members fill in to confirm their attendance and choose their meal.

This is set up by the **hall administrator** (or by the event organiser through the hall's admin area). If you would like one for your event, contact your hall administrator and ask them to enable it.

Once it is set up:

- A **"Book In for This Event"** button appears on the event's page on the website.
- A **QR code** is shown on the same page — members can scan it with their phone camera to go straight to the form.
- Share the event page link or QR code with your members.

Members fill in their name, contact details, masonic information, and (if a dinner menu has been added) their meal choice. Their responses are emailed to the addresses the organiser specified.

## Common questions

**I clicked Book but no form appeared.**
The date may already be fully booked, or the booking may only be available on the booking form page. Contact the hall administrator.

**I made a mistake in my booking.**
Use the **Update your booking** link in your confirmation email to correct it.

**I did not receive a confirmation email.**
Check your spam or junk folder. If it is not there, you may have typed your email address incorrectly. Contact the hall administrator to check whether the booking was received.

**I have lost my confirmation email.**
Contact the hall administrator. They can look up your booking and resend the edit or cancel links.

**My booking has been Pending for a long time.**
The administrator must approve it manually. Contact them directly if you are waiting for urgent confirmation.

**The date I want is showing as fully booked but I need that room.**
Contact the hall administrator — they may be able to adjust or move another booking.

**I cannot remember the booking password.**
Contact the hall administrator. They set the password in the system settings.
